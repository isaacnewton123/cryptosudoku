## 8.2 DAO Governance

Post-TGE, governance transitions to a **DAO**. SUDO holders (ERC-20 Votes) can
propose and vote on:

- Treasury allocation for development.
- Economic parameters (fees, rewards).
- Feature priorities.
- Strategic partnerships.
- Contract upgrade proposals (subject to multisig + 48h timelock).

This makes the platform **owned and directed by the community**, not a central team.

**Governance guardrails (refined):** quorum/thresholds, proposal timelock, and
emergency-pause authority should be specified before TGE — currently open (see Appendix).
